Local progress and deletion

Scores, medals, unlocked colors, settings, cumulative progress, recovery records and up to 50 recent completed runs are stored on your device. There is no supported cloud backup or automatic transfer to another phone, and we cannot restore saves from our servers.

In Settings → Run Data → Clear Run Data, the confirmed clear action deletes run history, not lifetime progress, medals or garage unlocks. Clearing the game's Android storage or uninstalling it removes local game data. Device loss or damage can also make a save unavailable. Keep this in mind before clearing storage, uninstalling or changing phones; these technical limits do not exclude responsibility imposed by law.
