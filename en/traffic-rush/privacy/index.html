Traffic Rush Privacy Policy

Scope and contact

This notice describes the no-ad-library Android builds 2.5.0 (version code 22) and 2.5.0-dev.14 (version code 12) of Traffic Rush, package com.galgengames.trafficrush. The controller for processing on our side is Vojtěch Müller (GalgenGames), Czech Republic. Contact us at galgengames@gmail.com for support and privacy requests.

These builds have no player account, advertising or consent SDK, ad-funded rescue, in-app purchases, chat, public leaderboard or cloud save. They do not send game progress or diagnostic records to our servers or advertising partners and have no remote game analytics. They do not ask for an advertising age group or advertising consent.

This description does not apply to earlier builds that contain advertising libraries, such as internal DEV.13, or to separate advertising QA applications. Those builds have different data practices. Turning off ad display in them is not the same as removing their SDKs. Before distributing advertising, purchases or cloud features, we will review their data handling and update the applicable information and choices.

Data kept on your device

The game stores your best score, medals, unlocked colors, settings, cumulative progress, recovery records and up to 50 completed runs in app storage. Run records include gameplay and performance information used by the local Run Data screen. These records support gameplay and saved progress; we cannot access them remotely. There is no automatic cloud transfer between phones.

After an update from an older build, previous local advertising settings may remain on the device, but these no-ad-library builds do not use them for advertising services. The game does not request your birth date, precise location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Retention and deletion

Local saves remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. History retains up to 50 recent runs while lifetime progress is kept separately. In Settings → Run Data → Clear Run Data, confirm the clear action to delete run history; it does not reset lifetime progress, medals or garage unlocks.

To remove all local game data, use Android's app settings for Traffic Rush and clear storage/data, or uninstall the app. Android labels vary. This removes saved progress, which we cannot restore from our own servers.

For personal information we hold, such as support correspondence, send a deletion request to galgengames@gmail.com. We can explain local deletion but cannot erase a phone remotely. Clearing app data does not erase messages already sent to us or information held independently by Google or another provider. We can direct you to those providers' tools, but do not promise deletion of all their records.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and resolve your request. Providing them is voluntary; we need a contact address to reply. Do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents. We do not use support addresses for marketing mailings.

Ordinary support relies on our legitimate interest in handling requested enquiries and fixing game problems, using only necessary information (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We delete correspondence within 12 months after the request is resolved, unless a specific legal obligation requires longer retention (Article 6(1)(c)). This maximum does not delay a valid deletion request. Gmail, provided by Google, handles the email service under its privacy policy.

Websites and other providers

The in-game privacy link opens an external browser; opening it does not upload your game save. Visiting the website involves its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, as described in our website privacy notice. Sending email, using Google Play and using Android are separate activities subject to the relevant providers' practices. They are not a promise that your phone never uses the internet.

Game data is stored in the app's local storage. Protect your phone with a screen lock and updates. No device or communication method is completely secure. Google may process support email outside your country; its transfer framework information explains applicable safeguards. Website hosting is addressed in the website notice.

Audience and your rights

Traffic Rush 2.5.0 is intended for players aged 16 and over in the EEA and the United Kingdom. Follow the regional content rating shown in Google Play. The game does not verify a player's age or identity.

Under applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction, object to legitimate-interest processing and request portability where its conditions apply. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. These no-ad-library builds do not request advertising consent. We do not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Contact galgengames@gmail.com about information under our control. You may complain to the relevant supervisory authority, including the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection. Future changes to processing will be reflected in an updated policy with its effective date.
