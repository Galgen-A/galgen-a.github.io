1. Who provides the game

The controller for processing on our side is Vojtěch Müller (GalgenGames), Czech Republic. For support and privacy enquiries, contact galgengames@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Traffic Rush Android app (com.galgengames.trafficrush) in its current test form. There is no registration, chat, public leaderboard, cloud save or in-app purchase. Visiting this website is covered separately by the website privacy notice.

2. Game data on your phone

The device stores scores, medals, unlocked colours, settings, temporary records used to recover results and up to 50 completed runs. These support gameplay and saved progress. We do not operate a server for these records and cannot access them remotely.

Variants with ad integration additionally store ad timing, your chosen age band and personalization preference; Google UMP maintains its own ad-choice state. After an update from an older version, previous local ad settings may remain stored, but the ad-free version does not use them for advertising services. We do not ask for a birth date, precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access. We do not send scores or run history to Google as ad parameters. We have no remote game analytics of our own and have not added Firebase Analytics.

3. Advertising and consent services

Only the variants with advertising libraries described above use Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and User Messaging Platform (UMP). When used, Google may receive IP addresses and inferred approximate location, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device/account identifiers, including advertising ID and App Set ID where available. Collection depends on the system, age protections and privacy choices. Purposes include ad delivery and measurement, operational analytics and fraud prevention. See Google's SDK data disclosure.

Advertising services may involve partners identified in the consent form. Their names, purposes and policy links are available in its details where the form is shown to you. Google's further processing is explained in its Privacy Policy and information for partner apps. Our code does not send your name or support email address to advertising services. Opening an advertiser's destination takes you to a service with its own practices.

4. Age band and consent

The game is intended for teenagers and adults, not children under 13. Suitability follows the store's regional age rating; the ad age selection does not change that rating or verify identity. For ads, you choose only a band: under 13, 13–15, 16–17 or 18 and older.

For unknown ages and the under-13 band, we do not start new ad or UMP requests. For minors we pass protective age signals and disable personalization. Adults can decline personalization or allow it only to the extent permitted by further valid consent. Choosing an age, continuing to play or using non-personalized ads does not mean consent to all processing.

In the debug version, ads are set up manually through Settings → Set Up Test Ads. UMP presents ad choices where applicable and the game respects its eligibility result. If setup fails, ads remain off. Declining must not prevent ordinary gameplay; depending on your choices, ads may be limited or unavailable, including an ad-funded rescue.

Use Settings → Ad Age Group to change the band and personalization preference. A change disables ads and clears loaded ad offers until a new setup. Where consent management must be offered, Settings → Ad Privacy Options lets you change or withdraw choices. An initialized SDK may remain in the app process; disabling ad display does not guarantee that all its network activity immediately stops.

5. Purposes and legal bases

Necessary local game storage provides the game you request. Age and consent settings respect your choices and protect minors. Consent is the basis for ad personalization and storage access where required (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)); you may withdraw it without affecting prior lawful processing.

Specific advertising purposes, recipients and any legitimate interests, such as secure ad delivery and fraud prevention, are described in the form and the providers' policies. Legitimate interest does not replace consent where consent is required. Our ordinary support relies on our legitimate interest in handling requested enquiries (Article 6(1)(f)). Mandatory retention follows the specific legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)). We do not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Local progress and settings: until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. History keeps up to 50 recent runs; older accumulated progress remains. Settings → Run Data clears run history, not all progress.
  • All local data: clear Traffic Rush's app data in Android settings or uninstall it. This also removes progress and saved ad choices. We do not offer our own cloud restore.
  • Support: no longer than 12 months after resolution, except where specific statutory retention applies.
  • Data held by Google and ad providers: retention depends on purposes, settings and their policies, including periods needed for service delivery, security or legal obligations. See Google's retention and deletion information. Our 12-month support limit does not apply to this category.

Uninstalling or clearing game data does not itself delete information already processed by another service. Depending on your Android version, you can manage the advertising ID in system ad settings; this is not the same as deleting all advertising records.

7. When you contact us

We process your email address, message and any attachments to handle your request. Providing them is voluntary; without a contact address we cannot reply. Gmail (Google) provides our email service. We delete resolved correspondence within 12 months unless a specific legal obligation requires longer retention. We do not use your address for marketing mailings. Do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents.

8. Security and international transfers

Game saves are kept in app storage on the device. Google encrypts ad-SDK data in transit using TLS. No device or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure; protect your phone with a lock and updates.

Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Depending on the circumstances, it describes adequacy decisions, the Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses as transfer safeguards. See Google's transfer framework information for details and access to information about safeguards. Visiting this page uses GitHub Pages as described in the website privacy notice.

9. Your rights

Under applicable law you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability, object to legitimate-interest processing and withdraw consent. Contact galgengames@gmail.com about our processing. We cannot access your game save remotely, but can explain local deletion. For providers' independent processing, you can also use their contacts and tools in the linked policies.

You may complain to your relevant supervisory authority, including the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection.

10. Changes to this policy

When processing changes, we will update this policy and its effective date. Before enabling live ads, purchases, cloud features or additional services, we will review the information and any new consent requirements. This document does not announce paid purchases or a public game release.
