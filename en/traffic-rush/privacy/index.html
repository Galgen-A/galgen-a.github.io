Traffic Rush Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · 2.5.1 /23 · 2.5.0 /22 & DEV.14 /12 · com.galgengames.trafficrush

Scope and contact

Traffic Rush is provided by Vojtěch Müller (GalgenGames), Czech Republic. He is the controller for processing on our side. Contact galgengames@gmail.com for support and privacy requests. The Android game's package is com.galgengames.trafficrush.

This notice describes the advertising-enabled Android update 2.5.1 /23 and distinguishes it from 2.5.0 /22 and DEV.14 /12. Those two no-ad-library builds have no advertising or consent SDK and do not use the advertising processing described below. Separate development QA apps are not the public Google Play release. The game has no player account, login, cloud save, chat, public leaderboard or in-app purchases.

Game data on your device

The game keeps scores, medals, unlocked colours, settings, lifetime progress, recovery records and up to 50 completed runs in private app storage. Run records include local gameplay and performance information for Run Data. Advertising variants also keep ad-frequency timing, your selected age band and personalisation preference. Google's consent tool keeps its own privacy-choice records separately.

We have no game-save server or remote access to these records. We do not upload your score or run history as our own advertising parameters or remote game analytics. Native durable storage preserves progress across restarts; a WebView compatibility copy may also exist. We do not provide cloud synchronisation. Android app backup is disabled in this build. These statements do not describe every manufacturer-provided device-transfer tool.

Advertising and Google services

Advertising variants use Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices. Google's services can process network addresses and broad location inferred from them, app/ad usage events, technical performance information and available app/device identifiers. We also send age-protection and advertising-preference signals and use the resulting consent signals. We do not request a birth date, GPS location, contacts, camera or microphone, or send support messages to advertising services.

These services deliver and measure advertising, analyse its operation, prevent fraud and apply privacy choices. Our configuration omits Android advertising-ID and AdServices permissions; this does not remove every possible identifier. We have not added a separate remote game-analytics service or mediation adapters. Other advertising partners may still participate through Google; the applicable Google privacy form identifies partners and their purposes. See Google's advertising SDK information, Google's privacy policy and information about partner apps.

Age group and advertising choices

Traffic Rush is intended for players 16 and over in the EEA and the United Kingdom. Follow the regional content rating on Google Play. The advertising age choice is not proof of age or identity and does not change that rating. It includes protective bands for younger users; this does not change the intended audience.

You can skip the initial age choice with Not now and still play. With no saved age, or an under-13 selection, our code does not start an explicit consent-update or ad-loading request. For minors, we apply protective age signals and disable personalisation. Adults can choose Do not personalize ads or allow personalisation only if their further consent permits it. Choosing an age band is not advertising consent.

After an eligible age is saved, each new launch automatically checks privacy choices and can prepare both advertising formats before you ask for a rescue. Where applicable, Google displays a form to accept, refuse or manage processing. The game remains playable after refusal, offline or if setup fails. It offers an optional rewarded rescue and occasional ads between runs, not banners during driving or an ad on launch. Availability is not guaranteed.

Refusal or a nonpersonalisation preference does not mean that all communication or processing stops. Ordinary nonpersonalised ads can still require consent to device storage. Where Google's checks permit it, limited advertising may remain possible after refusal; otherwise ads are skipped. Limited delivery can use an IP address for transmission and cache ad content. If programmatic limited ads are enabled, local storage can also support invalid-traffic detection. This is not a promise of zero collection. See Google's limited-ads explanation.

Use Settings → Ad Age Group to revise your age band and preference. Where available, Settings → Ad Privacy Options reopens Google's choices, including consent withdrawal. There is no permanent free switch disabling all advertising data collection. Not now stops the current setup but does not erase an existing age choice; a later launch can check again. Changing privacy choices invalidates loaded ads, not records already transmitted or an SDK already initialised in the process.

Purposes and legal bases

Necessary local storage provides the game you request. Age and privacy settings apply your choices and protect minors. Where required, advertising personalisation and non-essential storage access rely on consent. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Provider-specific purposes and any legitimate interests are explained in the applicable form and provider policies; legitimate interest does not replace required consent.

Our ordinary support uses only necessary information on the basis of our legitimate interest in handling requested enquiries and fixing game problems (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Any mandatory retention relies on the relevant legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)). We do not make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

Retention and deletion requests

Local saves remain until you clear app storage or uninstall. History retains up to 50 recent runs while lifetime progress is separate. Settings → Run Data → Clear Run Data clears run history only, not medals, unlocks, lifetime progress or advertising privacy choices. To remove all local data, use Android's app settings for Traffic Rush and clear storage/data, or uninstall. This loses progress; we cannot restore it from our own servers.

To request deletion of personal information under our control, email galgengames@gmail.com with the subject Traffic Rush privacy request and describe the information concerned. No game account or purchase is needed. Do not send passwords, identity documents or complete consent strings. We request only information necessary to locate and handle the request.

We handle records we hold and assess any necessary service-provider follow-up for processing on our behalf. We can explain local deletion but cannot erase your phone remotely. For independently managed Google/partner records, we can direct you to the provider's privacy tools and contacts. Uninstalling, withdrawing consent or changing an identifier does not delete all previously processed advertising records. Provider retention follows its purposes and policies, including Google's retention information; our support-email limit does not apply to those records.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your address, message and attachments you choose to send to answer and resolve the request. We use Gmail, provided by Google. A reply needs a contact address; providing it is voluntary. Do not send payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information. We do not use support addresses for marketing mailings.

We delete support correspondence within 12 months after resolution, unless a specific legal obligation requires longer. This maximum does not postpone a valid deletion request. It is our support-handling procedure, not an automatic deletion feature of the game or a retention promise for Google's advertising records.

Security and other providers

Game saves are in private app storage, not a promise of encrypted storage. Google documents TLS protection for its advertising SDK's transfers; this build also blocks cleartext HTTP. No system is completely secure. Protect your phone with a screen lock and updates.

Google and advertising partners may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Their applicable safeguards and processes are described in their policies; see Google's transfer information. Google Play, Android, an advertiser's destination and your email service also have their own practices.

The game's privacy link opens an external browser with a fixed address, not an upload of your game save. Website visits involve GitHub Pages hosting under our separate website privacy notice.

Your rights and changes

Under applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction, object to legitimate-interest processing, and request portability where its conditions apply. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact us about our processing; providers can also receive requests concerning their independent processing. You may complain to your supervisory authority, including the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection.

We will update this notice and its effective date when the processing changes, including before adding purchases, cloud features or materially different advertising services.
