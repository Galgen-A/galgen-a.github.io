Weave through traffic, spot the gaps and beat your best. Traffic Rush is an arcade driving game about quick reactions and getting just a little closer.

01 / ON THE ROAD

Every gap counts.

Close calls, a rising pace and busy streets. Stay alert, protect your armour and turn a well-timed move into more points.

02 / THE NEXT CHALLENGE

From bronze to gold.

Ten challenges, three tiers and 30 medals to earn. Improve your single runs and lifetime stats. Gold takes practice.

03 / YOUR PERSONAL BEST

Play. Improve. Repeat.

No sign-up. Scores, medals and unlocked colours stay on your device. The driving game works offline, too.

Your car. Your colours.

Good driving.
Great style.

Unlock new colours by reaching higher scores. More car designs are in the works — there are no in-app purchases yet.

Behind the wheel at GalgenGames

A small indie project.
A love for the little things.

I'm Vojtěch Müller, the developer behind GalgenGames and Traffic Rush. What started as a browser game is becoming a mobile arcade experience.

Right now, I'm polishing the Android version: performance, controls and the details that make you want another run. Feedback is always welcome.

The game includes stylised collisions with vehicles and cyclists and is not intended for children under 13. Age ratings vary by region: PEGI 16 in Europe, ESRB Teen in North America and PG in Australia. Follow your local rating. The Android test build includes optional rewarded rescue ads and occasional ads between runs; only test ads are currently used.
