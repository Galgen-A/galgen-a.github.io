Website privacy

This page covers only the information website and email contact. It is not the privacy policy for the Traffic Rush mobile app. For the game, read the separate app policy.

1. Operator and scope

The GalgenGames website is operated by Vojtěch Müller (GalgenGames), the controller for data processed on our side when handling messages. For support and privacy questions, contact galgengames@gmail.com.

This website introduces Traffic Rush, a game in development. You do not sign into the game, play it or make purchases on this website.

2. Visiting the site

We have not added analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, cookies of our own or contact forms. Images and styles are served from this website, without external fonts or embedded social networks. No account is required.

Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for service security. This is hosting-related processing, not our own visitor analytics. Its further practices, retention periods, processing outside your country and safeguards for international transfers are described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We cannot delete hosting logs on GitHub's behalf.

The public site uses encrypted HTTPS. Linked third-party websites follow their respective operators' policies.

3. When you email us

An email link only opens your email app; no message is sent automatically. If you write to us, we process your email address, message and any attachments to resolve your request and follow up. Providing data is voluntary; without a contact address we cannot reply. Do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents.

For ordinary enquiries, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to requested communications and resolving user issues, limited to necessary data (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We do not use your address for marketing mailings or automated decision-making about you.

Correspondence is kept for no longer than 12 months after the request is resolved and is then deleted, unless a specific legal obligation requires longer retention. In that case, we keep only the necessary data for the required period based on that obligation (GDPR Article 6(1)(c)).

Our Gmail service is provided by Google, which also processes messages and related technical data in operating the service. For recipients, security, retention and potential transfers outside the European Economic Area and their safeguards, see the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your rights

Under applicable law, you may request access, correction or erasure of data and restriction of processing, and portability where its legal conditions apply. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation.

Send requests to galgengames@gmail.com. For processing for which GitHub or Google are independently responsible, you can also use their contacts in the linked policies. You may complain to the relevant supervisory authority. In the Czech Republic, this is the Office for Personal Data Protection.

5. Changes to this information

If the website or our handling of correspondence changes, we will update this text and its effective date. The mobile game's policy will be a separate document based on the version of the app actually released.
